Fix typos, grammar and tone in any text field with AI. Uses your own OpenRouter API key.

AI Message Enhancer polishes what you write in chats, emails and any other text field. It fixes typos and grammar, rewrites awkward or word-for-word translated phrasing into clear, plain English that non-native readers understand, and adjusts the tone, while keeping your meaning and your language.

Write your message as usual, then click the Enhance button next to the field, press Alt+Shift+E or right-click and choose "Enhance with AI Message Enhancer". Review the result in a preview, press Replace to put it in the field, and undo it with one click if you change your mind. You can also paste text into the toolbar popup and copy the result.

Key Features:
- Enhance in place: works in text fields and rich editors on the web, including chat and email apps. Select part of the text to enhance only that part.
- Undo: one click (or Ctrl+Z) brings back your original text.
- Tone and format: a relaxed Neutral tone or a professional (not stiff) Formal tone, chat message or email format.
- Translation: optionally translate your text to English while enhancing it.
- Your choice of model: Grok 4.3 by default, or any other model available on OpenRouter, with a one-click connection test.
- Your choice of site: hide the in-page button on sites you choose.
- Light and dark themes.

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- You need your own OpenRouter API key. Usage is billed by OpenRouter to your account.
- Your text is sent only when you ask for an enhancement, and only to the provider of the model you chose. It is never sent to the developer.
- API keys are stored on your device only.
