AI Message Enhancer Settings

How your text is handled

When you press Enhance in the popup, click ✨ Enhance next to a text field, press the keyboard shortcut or use the right-click menu, the text of that field (or your selection) is sent with your API key to the provider of the model you chose: OpenRouter, which forwards it to the model's maker (xAI, Google, OpenAI or Anthropic), or OpenAI directly. Nothing is sent before one of those actions, and nothing is ever sent to the extension's developer.

When enabled, your input and enhanced text will be saved and restored when you reopen the extension.

The button opens a preview of the enhanced text (or of the selected part). Nothing in the page changes until you press Replace, and Undo puts it back.
