AI Message Enhancer Settings

When enabled, your input and enhanced text will be saved and restored when you reopen the extension.

When enabled, a ✨ icon appears in text fields on websites like Gmail, Slack and Teams. Click it to enhance the text in place (or only the selected part), with an Undo option. The keyboard shortcut and the right-click menu work even when the icon is hidden. Change the shortcut (default Alt+Shift+E) at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

When enabled, request timings are logged to the extension's service worker console.

The ✨ icon will be hidden on these domains. Use full domains (github.com) to block entire sites, include paths (github.com/user/repo) to block specific pages, or specify ports (localhost:3000) for local development.
